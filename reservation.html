Table Reservation & Services

Plan your visit in advance to enjoy a smooth dining experience with us.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a table?

We recommend reserving at least 24 hours in advance for weekdays and 48 hours for weekend evening dining.

Is there a dress code at Buddies Restaurant?

Our dress code is smart casual. We want our guests to feel comfortable while maintaining an elegant atmosphere.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?

Yes! Our chefs can adapt most dishes for vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free diets. Please inform us when reserving.

How to Reserve Your Table

  1. Choose your preferred date and time slot.
  2. Select the number of guests in your party.
  3. Contact our reception via phone or the online form.
  4. Receive an instant confirmation email or SMS.

Included Dining Services

  • Complimentary welcome drinks for reserved guests
  • Dedicated VIP seating options upon request
  • Customized birthday and anniversary table decor
  • Valet parking service at the main entrance
